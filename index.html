Weave math

Every weaving draft says "sett at 12 epi, warp 3 yards" and stops there. Whether your yarn wants 8 or 24 ends, and whether the cone covers the weft, is multiplication - and the draft never shows it.

Measure before you wind

The problem it solves

Sett is not a suggestion from the pattern; it falls out of your yarn. Wrap it around a ruler, and plain weave wants about half those wraps per inch, twill two-thirds, satin three-quarters. Wind the wrong sett and the cloth comes off the loom as cardboard or gauze.

Then the warp itself: ends from width and sett, length from the woven piece plus fringe, take-up, shrinkage and the honest 27 inches of loom waste nobody budgets. And the weft: picks times width, with draw-in. Weave math runs all of it - sett from your actual yarn, warp yardage with the waste included, and a weft check against what is on your shelf.
